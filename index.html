About

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Southern California, working on development economics, international trade, and public finance, with a regional focus on Uganda. My research uses firm-level administrative and survey data to study intermediation, supply chain disruptions, and tax compliance among Ugandan firms. I hold an MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics and a BComm in Economics from Yonsei University.
